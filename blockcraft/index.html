What's new

Survival

Punch trees, craft planks, sticks and a crafting table, then wooden and stone tools. Mining takes time and needs the right tool. Watch your health, hunger and air, and get your items back after you die.

Developer toolkit

Press / or T in a world for commands and F4 for the dev panel: time of day, teleport, find caves, give items, god mode, no-clip, chunk borders and an inspector. Turn it off under Settings.

In development

Light and torches, a new biome world generator, furnaces and chests, then creatures and combat. See the roadmap in the project docs.
